# Data licence

The compiled dataset in `data/snapshots/` and `data/latest/` is released under
the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Suggested attribution: "vtaircrafts.in, compiled from DGCA operator lists".

Source material: Directorate General of Civil Aviation, Government of India,
lists of scheduled and non-scheduled operators. Reproduced with acknowledgement
under the DGCA website policy. The original PDFs are kept unmodified in
`data/raw/` with their SHA-256 hashes recorded in each snapshot's `meta.json`.

ICAO 24-bit addresses and type designators are looked up in the readsb aircraft
database maintained by Mictronics and distributed through tar1090-db.

Image metadata points to photographs on Wikimedia Commons. Each photograph keeps
its own licence and photographer credit, recorded per record.

This is an independent, unofficial index. It is not the Indian civil aircraft
register and must not be relied on for operational or legal purposes.
